The product is always written “nujli.”, tagline “Impact Revealed.” Humanitarian organizations pay; donors browse without accounts.

Never invent humanitarian projects, statistics, photos, videos, testimonials, payment confirmations or generation success. Empty public states are intentional. Test fixtures must be labeled SAMPLE and must not become donor content.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires the real product rather than a mockup and rejects untested success claims.

**How to apply:** Final acceptance reports use PASS, FAIL, BLOCKED, NOT TESTED. Live Whop payment acceptance remains blocked until a purchase and entitlement check have actually run. Preserve the existing Whop product; never silently replace it or hardcode its price.

The owner may use an explicitly granted developer-access button to access organization tools without paying in development. This is not a purchase or a verified membership.

**Why:** The user requested personal developer access to the inner organization experience without payment.

**How to apply:** Require the signed-in, server-authorized development account, visibly label developer status, leave ordinary users' Whop gates intact, disable this privilege in production, and do not extend independent-review permissions.

Do not add the “Just browsing” donor shortcut to the landing page.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested its removal; their immediate need was access to the organization side.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing “Show the Evidence” role selection and provide the owner a direct organization-workspace link when they ask for organization access.

Funding simulation must remain distinct from documented construction. AI extraction is not independent review. Evidence labels are exactly Organization Reported, Corroborated, Independently Reviewed.

**Why:** Donors must not confuse simulated funding, organization assertions or AI checks with real construction or independent verification.

**How to apply:** Only saved authorized independent reviews can produce Independently Reviewed. Unpublished drafts remain private. Avoid personal beneficiary information and use coarse public locations.

The organization experience has five visible stages: Choose, Pay, Generate, Review, Publish. AI builds the timeline, statistics and page; organizations correct and approve them rather than assembling reports manually. The workspace is “My Projects”, not a CRM, and has no analytics.

**Why:** The user explicitly requires a simple organization journey with no repeated forms or jargon.

**How to apply:** Progress must follow persisted backend work, success must follow a saved publication, and a second physical-device test must not be confused with responsive browser emulation. Keep one predefined conceptual Visual Build model; do not promise custom 3D models.

The user requests a Falastin-inspired visual identity and initial map view around Palestine and its surrounding region, with no borders or political lines. Keep the supplied logos, restrained tatreez details, English fonts, and Arabic language/direction markup. The Arabic tagline requires fluent-speaker review.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose this identity and geographic starting point; it is not evidence of a project's location or activity.

**How to apply:** Only actual published project coordinates produce markers. Use red eight-point stars for map markers; do not populate an empty regional map with fictional projects.

Use the owner's exact logo SVG assets when available. The dark version is primary. The Palestinian-flag period must have no outline or ring; its top stripe is soft charcoal on dark backgrounds and true black on light backgrounds.

**Why:** The user explicitly said “I dont like the white outline around the period” and “love this. esp the dark”.

**How to apply:** Preserve supplied artwork rather than substituting font text, and use the alt text “nujli.” Keep the landing headline's haze limited to “deserve”, static and readable.
